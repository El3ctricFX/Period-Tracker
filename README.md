## Instruction

so whenever her period starts you would track it, the way i do it is inside of Google Sheets and i set a thing for each date of each month and on the days of bleeding (menstrual) you would put a 1 and leave the rest blank and you could just do this any other way but its how i do. but then after we know its over that is when you would put it into this website / tracker / prediction whatever you wanna call it and then it will give you the information you need (the more information you give it the more accurate it will be and obviously wont be perfect and wont even be good at the start because it wont have data to go off of but i found its accurate a lot of the time but obviously the human body is weird and sometimes is completely off but is overall good for what it is)

and the way it keeps track of the data is through local storage so if you go to clear your cookies your dates **will** be gone and so the way i use it is through json and on the left hand side you'll see "edit raw data" and so after you enter your dates of first day to end day you **will** and should if your using this back up that string of json code so that whenever u want to import it you just paste it right into there and boom it works
